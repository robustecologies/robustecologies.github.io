# tuRbulence: Stochastic chaos and turbulent attractors in dynamical systems

Implements stochastic dynamical systems for studying turbulent
attractors with Ornstein-Uhlenbeck colored noise forcing, with emphasis
on Earth system models and ocean-atmosphere interactions. Includes seven
systems: von Karman turbulent flow in the stochastic Duffing oscillator,
Lorenz-63 and Lorenz-84, Rössler, Stommel thermohaline circulation,
Charney-DeVore atmospheric blocking, and Hasselmann stochastic climate
model. Provides attractor reconstruction via delay and peak embedding,
Lyapunov exponent estimation (Wolf and Rosenstein algorithms), embedding
dimension estimation (Cao's method), and interactive 3D visualization of
chaotic attractors. Streamlined with Rcpp and C++/OpenMP for
computational efficiency.
