# Package index

## Enumeration

The twenty-five principles as a documented data object, carrying the
structural partition of Samkhya-Karika 3, the immediate material cause
of each principle, and its membership in the internal organ, the
elevenfold set and the subtle body. Every table, diagram and method in
the package derives from this object, so the numbering cannot drift
between them.

- [`tattvas`](https://robustecologies.github.io/SamkhyaR/reference/tattvas.md)
  : The twenty-five tattvas of the Samkhya enumeration

## Evolution

The branching derivation of Samkhya-Karika 22 and 25. The eleven organs
issue from the sattvic aspect of ahamkara and the five tanmatras from
its tamasic aspect, in parallel rather than in sequence, and the gross
elements descend from the tanmatras alone. Each step enforces its own
causal precondition and preserves the guna triple on the two-simplex.

- [`init_prakriti()`](https://robustecologies.github.io/SamkhyaR/reference/init_prakriti.md)
  : Initialise Prakrti in its primordial equilibrium
- [`evolution_buddhi()`](https://robustecologies.github.io/SamkhyaR/reference/evolution_buddhi.md)
  : Evolve buddhi, the cosmic intellect
- [`evolution_ahamkara()`](https://robustecologies.github.io/SamkhyaR/reference/evolution_ahamkara.md)
  : Evolve ahamkara, the principle of self-appropriation
- [`evolution_manas()`](https://robustecologies.github.io/SamkhyaR/reference/evolution_manas.md)
  : Evolve manas, the eleventh organ
- [`evolution_indriyas()`](https://robustecologies.github.io/SamkhyaR/reference/evolution_indriyas.md)
  : Evolve the ten organs of knowledge and action
- [`evolution_tanmatras()`](https://robustecologies.github.io/SamkhyaR/reference/evolution_tanmatras.md)
  : Evolve the five tanmatras
- [`evolution_mahabhutas()`](https://robustecologies.github.io/SamkhyaR/reference/evolution_mahabhutas.md)
  : Evolve the five gross elements
- [`samkhya_evolve()`](https://robustecologies.github.io/SamkhyaR/reference/samkhya_evolve.md)
  : Run the complete evolution of Prakrti

## Liberation

The cessation of Prakrti’s activity once discriminative knowledge has
arisen in buddhi. Samkhya-Karika 62 denies that anyone is bound,
released or a transmigrant, so nothing here alters Purusha.

- [`get_kaivalya()`](https://robustecologies.github.io/SamkhyaR/reference/get_kaivalya.md)
  : Attain kaivalya through discriminative knowledge

## Doctrinal reference

Reference objects for the doctrines that surround the evolution: the
three means of valid knowledge in the karika’s own vocabulary, the eight
dispositions of buddhi with the fiftyfold intellectual creation, the
eighteen constituents of the subtle body, and the five arguments for the
pre-existence of the effect in its cause.

- [`samkhya_pramanas()`](https://robustecologies.github.io/SamkhyaR/reference/samkhya_pramanas.md)
  : The three means of valid knowledge
- [`buddhi_bhavas()`](https://robustecologies.github.io/SamkhyaR/reference/buddhi_bhavas.md)
  : The eight dispositions of buddhi and the fiftyfold intellectual
  creation
- [`linga_sharira()`](https://robustecologies.github.io/SamkhyaR/reference/linga_sharira.md)
  : The subtle body and its eighteen constituents
- [`satkaryavada()`](https://robustecologies.github.io/SamkhyaR/reference/satkaryavada.md)
  : The doctrine that the effect pre-exists in its cause
