# Demon choice data set

This data set is for discrete choice models and consists of the choice
of commuting route to school: arterial, two-lane, or freeway. There were
151 Pennsylvania commuters who started from a residential complex in
State College, PA, and commute to downtown State College.

## Format

A data frame with 151 rows and 9 columns:

- Choice:

  Route choice: four-lane arterial (35 MPH), two-lane highway (35 MPH),
  or limited-access four-lane freeway (55 MPH).

- HH.Income:

  Ordinal annual household income (1: \<20k, 2: 20-30k, 3: 30-40k, 4:
  40k+ USD).

- Vehicle.Age:

  Age in years of the commuter's vehicle.

- Stop.Signs.Arterial:

  Number of stop signs along the arterial.

- Stop.Signs.Two.Lane:

  Number of stop signs along the two-lane.

- Stop.Signs.Freeway:

  Number of stop signs along the freeway.

- Distance.Arterial:

  Distance in miles of the arterial route.

- Distance.Two.Lane:

  Distance in miles of the two-lane route.

- Distance.Freeway:

  Distance in miles of the freeway route.

## Source

Washington, S., Congdon, P., Karlaftis, M., and Mannering, F. (2009).
