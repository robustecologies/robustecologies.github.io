# Demon snacks data set

Nutritional attributes for 39 snack items, each with a name and 10
nutritional measurements.

## Format

A data frame with 39 rows (snack items, named) and 10 columns:

- Serving.Size:

  Serving size in grams.

- Calories:

  Number of calories.

- Total.Fat:

  Total fat in grams.

- Saturated.Fat:

  Saturated fat in grams.

- Cholesterol:

  Cholesterol in milligrams.

- Sodium:

  Sodium in milligrams.

- Total.Carbohydrate:

  Total carbohydrates in grams.

- Dietary.Fiber:

  Dietary fiber in grams.

- Sugars:

  Sugar in grams.

- Protein:

  Protein in grams.

## Source

This data was obtained from the lair of Lucifer.
