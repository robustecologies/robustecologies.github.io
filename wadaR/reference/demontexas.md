# Demon space-time data set

Temperature data for space-time models requiring latitude and longitude
coordinates. Contains minimum, mean, and maximum temperatures at 369
sites in Texas for 13 months.

## Format

A data frame with 369 rows (sites) and 43 columns:

- Elevation:

  Elevation of the site.

- Latitude:

  Latitude of the site.

- Longitude:

  Longitude of the site.

- Gulf:

  Gulf indicator for the site.

- Max1, Max2, Max3, Max4, Max5, Max6, Max7, Max8, Max9, Max10, Max11,
  Max12, Max13:

  Maximum temperature for months 1 through 13.

- Mean1, Mean2, Mean3, Mean4, Mean5, Mean6, Mean7, Mean8, Mean9, Mean10,
  Mean11, Mean12, Mean13:

  Mean temperature for months 1 through 13.

- Min1, Min2, Min3, Min4, Min5, Min6, Min7, Min8, Min9, Min10, Min11,
  Min12, Min13:

  Minimum temperature for months 1 through 13.
