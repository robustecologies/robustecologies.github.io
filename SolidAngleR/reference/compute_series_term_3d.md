# Compute a single term in the 3D hypergeometric series

Computes the term corresponding to multiexponent (a12, a13, a23).

## Usage

``` r
compute_series_term_3d(a12, a13, a23, alpha_12, alpha_13, alpha_23)
```

## Arguments

- a12:

  Exponent for \\\alpha\_{12}\\

- a13:

  Exponent for \\\alpha\_{13}\\

- a23:

  Exponent for \\\alpha\_{23}\\

- alpha_12:

  Dot product \\v_1 \cdot v_2\\

- alpha_13:

  Dot product \\v_1 \cdot v_3\\

- alpha_23:

  Dot product \\v_2 \cdot v_3\\

## Value

The term value
